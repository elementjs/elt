### U27. The scheme classes do not set `color-scheme`

**[read]** The light, dark and dynamic scheme classes of elt/ui (`ui/theme.tsx`) do not set the CSS `color-scheme` property. > **b)** No.
> ➡️(a) Light scrollbars on a dark page are a visible defect, and it removes the special case in the docs.

### U28. `packed` counts hidden children

`hidden` now works on every element: the `reset` layer has `[hidden]:not([hidden="until-found"]) { display: none !important }` (an important declaration in the first layer wins over every later layer; `until-found` stays the browser's, so the content stays searchable). Test `tests/reset.pw.ts` (fails without the rule). `ui-layout.md` says so. Before, `hidden` did nothing on `e-prose`, `e-row`, `e-column`, `e-flex`, `e-grid`, `button`, `input`: the browser's own `[hidden]` rule loses to any page rule that sets `display`.

Left as you asked: `packed` picks its outer children with `:first-child` / `:last-child`, so a hidden child still counts: when the last child is hidden, the visible last child loses its bottom border and corners. `ui-layout.md` lists it as a limitation (use `If` instead of `hidden`). The `select.tsx` workaround (`& > input[hidden] { display: none }`) is removed.

> ❓ **Question**: Do we make `packed` skip hidden children?
> **a)** Yes: `:nth-child(1 of :not([hidden]))` and `:nth-last-child(1 of :not([hidden]))` instead of `:first-child` / `:last-child` in the packed rules.
> **b)** No: keep the limitation.
> ➡️(a) Later, when you want to touch these selectors.
